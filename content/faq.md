[[extra.faqs]]
q = "Will this make my work sound like a robot?"
a = "No — and if it did, I would have failed. The setup is built on your own writing, your own voice, your standards. What gets removed is the tedium: the fourth draft of the same email, the listing description you have written two hundred times. The judgment stays yours. It has to; it is the only thing anyone is paying you for."

[[extra.faqs]]
q = "I tried the chatbot and it was underwhelming."
a = "That is the common experience, and it is not your fault. Typing questions into a blank box is roughly like being handed a Unix prompt in 1994 — enormous capability, no idea where to start. The value is not in the box. It is in wiring the thing into the specific places your work slows down, which takes someone sitting beside you who has done it before."

[[extra.faqs]]
q = "Do I have to buy software, or move my business onto something new?"
a = "No. I build around what you already use. There is no platform to migrate to, no subscription of mine to maintain, and nothing that stops working if we part ways. You keep everything we make. That is not a promise made to sound generous — it is how I have always believed tools should be built."

[[extra.faqs]]
q = "Are you going to tell me to replace my staff?"
a = "I am not. Small operations are not overstaffed — they are overextended. Every business I have sat with has more work than hands, and the useful question is which work should have hands on it at all. Nobody hired anyone to write meta descriptions."

[[extra.faqs]]
q = "Why should I trust you with how my business works?"
a = "Because I run one. Two, in fact — a contemporary gallery and a letterpress imprint, both in a small town in New Mexico. I have spent thirty years around computers and twenty around ink and paper, and I have never once believed those were different kinds of craft."
